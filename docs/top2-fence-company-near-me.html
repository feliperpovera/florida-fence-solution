02What the search results show

Local intent. Google rewrites "near me" into the searcher's city, so the real fight is "fence company in Hollywood, FL" (and the same for each Broward city). The organic results are split: 4 directories (Yelp, Thumbtack, Houzz, HomeGuide) and 4 contractors. That split is an opening: Google is unsure whether people want a list or a company.

  • Ads on top: the client, XL Fencing, Bulldog Fence and Angi.
  • Map pack: The Hollywood Fence Company (3 reviews), Mega Fence (11), Exclusive Custom Fence (110). The client shows only as a sponsored listing (21 reviews, 5.0).
  • "People also ask": three of four questions are about cost ("average cost of a fence", "200 ft of fencing", "how much should I pay"). No contractor in the top results answers this with real numbers.
  • No AI Overview on this query today. In Google AI Mode the client is already named for "fence company near me", Durafence and Spanish-speaking queries, but not for "best fence company near Hollywood".

05Our entry angle

Build authority: local field + long tail + multiformat

Both leaders score as solid, and the authority gap is high: Superior has 707 reviews and a national brand, and the map-pack leaders have up to 110 reviews against the client's 21. A head-on attack on Yelp is not realistic now. The method's rule for "solid competitor + high authority gap" is to build authority from the flanks.

  • Local field (main lever). For "near me", the map pack and AI Mode read Google Business Profile: reviews, services, photos, warranty, hours. This is where the client can win first.
  • Long tail. City pages and service pages already exist. Strengthen the ones with impressions and no clicks ("fence installation hollywood fl", "wood fence company south florida", "fence repair fort lauderdale").
  • Multiformat. Real job photos and one short install video (Durafence or PVC) on YouTube and the site.
  • Prerequisite: fix speed. The client fails the Largest Contentful Paint test while both leaders pass, so on an equal-relevance tie the client loses.

09Limits of this analysis

  • Google does not publish ranking weights. What the top two have in common does not prove it causes their rank.
  • The SERP was captured in a signed-in browser with personalization off and the location set to Hollywood, FL (a clean browser was blocked by a CAPTCHA). Results can differ by device and exact location.
  • Yelp blocks automated reading, so its on-page review is based on the search result and is partial.
  • Links were verified by our bot on candidate pages only, so the lists are partial. We don't use DR or DA scores.
  • AI visibility was tested in Google AI Mode only, 4 queries, one run each. ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity were not tested (they need a login). This is a starting point, not a full GEO measurement.
  • No timelines or positions are guaranteed. The plan measures direction.
